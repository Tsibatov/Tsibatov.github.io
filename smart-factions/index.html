What changes in your campaign

01

They remember

Every faction keeps track of what you promised, what you delivered and what you did behind their back. Last war's betrayal comes up at this war's negotiating table.

02

They lie

Leaders bluff, flatter and make offers they never meant to keep. Read the message carefully — then watch the border.

03

Deals become armies

An alliance, a joint war or a quiet pact isn't flavour text. Agreements turn into actual orders: armies march, sieges start, help arrives — or it doesn't.
