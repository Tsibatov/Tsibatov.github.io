Worldbuilding · simulation · Unreal Engine 5.8 plugin

Water Dynamics

Rivers, lakes and waterfalls that respond to what is in them. Rocks change the flow, and the flow moves the surface, the foam and the wet line on the banks.

Problem

River surfaces are usually driven by a hand-painted flow map. It looks right until someone moves a rock, and then the art and the flow disagree.

Approach

  • A spline defines the channel. A depth-averaged finite-volume shallow-water solver computes the flow (hydrostatic bed reconstruction, CFL substeps, Manning friction).
  • Rock collision is written into the bed height map: exposed rocks split the current, submerged rocks make it shallower.
  • The solved velocity drives ripples, advected foam and buoyancy, so one field feeds every visual layer.
  • Waterfalls are gravity sheets with momentum forcing into the pool. Stone impacts add a wave packet, a splash crown, a rebound jet and droplets.
  • Single Layer Water optics: IOR and Fresnel, depth absorption, refraction and approximate caustics.
  • A wet-line material function follows the local water level, so banks and rocks darken where the water reaches and dry again over time.

For the artist

One panel creates a river, lake or waterfall. Width, depth and flow speed are in real units. The artist switches between realistic and stylized looks, picks a waterbed (rocky, sandy, silty, algae), sets turbidity, and saves the result as a look preset for other water bodies.

Scope: authoring beta. 2.5D surface flow inside a finite domain, not a 3D FLIP or SPH volume. Profiled in the editor at 1080p and not yet tuned to a shipping budget. Showcase scenery is Quixel Megascans via Fab, used in the demo only and not shipped with the plugin.

Performance Regression Companion

Problem

Content changes are usually judged by watching the frame counter in the editor. Single runs are noisy, editor overhead varies, and two people can measure the same change and disagree.

Approach

  • A saved test profile fixes the map, camera, resolution, quality settings and frame budget.
  • Every repeat launches a separate Unreal process and records Epic's native CSV Profiler data. Unreal Insights traces are optional.
  • Five repeats per content version. The comparison checks engine, hardware, camera and settings before it makes a claim, and differences inside the observed noise stay marked as uncertain.
  • Results export as a portable HTML/JSON report with the raw captures, so a reviewer can check the analysis without Unreal.

Result

In a dense scene of liquid props, the captures localized game-thread time spent rebuilding foam meshes. Reducing that geometry detail brought frame p95 from 47.2 to 15.0 ms and game-thread p95 from 46.6 to 14.9 ms.

On real content

To exercise the workflow on a production-like scene, I rebuilt the Lyra Expanse environment (2,391 placed mesh components, 277 meshes, 21 materials) as three ordinary levels with 0, 12 and 48 added shadow-casting lights.

The 48-light setup exposed a Virtual Shadow Map packed-light overflow. Raising the per-pixel limit was not enough; the fix was to hold r.Shadow.Virtual.OnePassProjection=0 identical across baseline and candidate, as a correctness choice rather than an optimization.

Measurements on this scene are still in progress. No speed-up is claimed for it.

Worldbuilding · artist-facing tool

Weather Dynamics and Weather Studio

Seasonal weather an artist can add to an existing level from one panel, without breaking its lighting.

Problem

Weather systems are often owned by an engineer, and adding one to a lit level tends to replace its sky and exposure. Artists need presets, a live preview and safe defaults.

Approach

  • One editor panel with three setup modes: weather only, clouds only, or both. Existing lighting is preserved; adding missing lighting is an explicit action.
  • 12 weather presets and 8 cloud looks stored as reusable data assets, with four quality tiers.
  • Rain, snow and autumn leaves accumulate on surfaces through finite heightfield patches, with a material function for receiver wetness.
  • Deep snow: a Character component leaves body troughs, footprints and raised banks that persist and refill under snowfall.

Scope: finite static patches. No automatic Landscape binding, open-world coverage or network replication yet.

Trestle Bridge

Draw a path across a ravine, and the structure fits the terrain.

Approach

  • A NURBS curve and a terrain mesh are the only inputs. Sliders set deck width, support spacing, tier height and path coverage; editing the curve rebuilds the structure and updates terrain contacts.
  • Bents are placed along the path and their legs find the ground with raycasts. When a raycast misses, the tool reports the location instead of hiding the gap, so the artist fixes the layout rather than the geometry.
  • Output is eight organized mesh batches (decking, stringers, frames, bracing, hardware), with custom component meshes and seeded presets.
  • Each member carries a vertex-colour payload as shader data, which gives the reference deck 158 distinct board variations without a material per board. Verified by reading the Unreal import back into Maya.

The live generator runs in Maya; Unreal receives baked static meshes. The video is an edited capture, not a real-time performance test.

Character material studies

Three Fortnite outfit looks rebuilt as materials from public video previews alone. No extracted assets and no textures: the effects are HLSL inside Custom nodes, and each project regenerates its materials from a Python script.

Reference: Grimey

Ink Souls

  • 12 procedural symbols and a persistent face, drawn mathematically on Epic's Manny.
  • The inner pattern turns toward the camera, so it reads as depth inside the body rather than a decal.
  • One opaque Default Lit material. Height, mapping yaw and offset parameters port it to other skeletal meshes.

Reference: Cyclo

Storm Helmet

  • The helmet surface integrates swirling clouds and a glowing core inside an ellipsoid volume (8–40 steps, 24 by default).
  • Arms carry moving volumetric noise with thin pulsing discharges; a separate glass dome adds Fresnel and highlight.
  • Built on my own procedural armored mannequin.

  • Iridescent bones whose hue shifts at grazing angles, under a translucent shell with a rim boost.

Shaders · physics · Unreal Engine 5.8 plugin

Liquid Props

Liquid in bottles and glasses that keeps its volume when tilted and reacts to motion and impacts.

Approach

  • A gravity-aligned free surface whose height is solved so the liquid keeps its volume under tilt. Per-frame telemetry records the surface normal and volume error.
  • Sloshing responds to translation and impulses, with artist-facing damping and viscosity.
  • Four recipes (wine, still water, cola, sparkling water) and four container shapes: carbonation bubbles, a foam head that forms with motion and decays, condensation and wall film.
  • Everything is a property on the actor: fill, tint, opacity, damping, viscosity, bubbles and foam. Artists never touch C++.

Connection

The performance case came from this project: the Companion localized the foam-mesh rebuild that dominated the game thread in a dense scene of these props.

Scope: a planar sloshing surface with approximate absorption and artistic viscosity, not a general fluid solver. The video uses a fixed simulation step and is not a frame-rate claim.

Editor tools · Unreal Engine 5.8

Pipeline tools

Smaller tools for the same goal: problems found where content is made, with a report the artist can act on alone.

Available on Fab · v1.1

Blueprint Logic Scanner

Exports Blueprints, materials, data tables, curve tables, enums and structs to JSON, Markdown and a compact text summary, with a cross-reference pass between them. Used for reviews, documentation and AI-assisted debugging; nothing is sent anywhere automatically. A headless commandlet runs batch scans.

v1.1

Project Audit

Collects budget and naming violations in one list: texture size and memory, power-of-two, vertex and triangle limits, asset prefixes. Profiles are shareable files, the scan never modifies assets, and every row opens the offending asset. Exports a PASS/FAIL report.

v0.1 beta

Material Batch Studio

Creates or updates material instances from imported texture sets in one pass. Every change and error is previewed per set before anything is written; skip and update policies protect existing work.

v0.3

SurfaceFusion

One master material for rock, ground and concrete. Texture scale, moss, dirt, wetness, snow and ground contact are instance parameters, with 10 presets and 10 material functions. Plugs into Weather Dynamics so surfaces react to the current weather.
